# Team Builder

**Front Desk Staff Copilot**

---

## What It Is

A tablet-optimized tool that helps YMCA front-desk staff match families to programs and complete registration on the spot. It runs in two modes: a warm, visual guided flow when a family is standing at the desk, and a fast search dashboard when staff need to look something up quickly.

## Who It's For

- **Front-desk and phone staff** at YMCA branches
- **Families at the counter** (via the guided flow mode the staff can turn toward them)

## How It Works

### Mode A — Guided Flow (Family-Facing)

1. **Welcome** — "What's your child's name?" (personalizes the whole experience)
2. **Age** — Visual card selection (5-6 through 13+)
3. **Interests** — Pick from 6 activity categories with icons and descriptions
4. **Results** — Matched programs in a browsable grid
5. **Confirm & register** — Staff fills in family details, adds notes for the coach, and enrolls

### Mode B — Quick Search (Staff Dashboard)

- Full-text search across programs, sports, branches, and age groups
- Filter chips for age ranges and activity types
- Compact 6-column results table (program, location, ages, spots, schedule, tags)
- One click to jump to registration

### After Selection (Both Modes)

- **Program summary** with real-time spot availability (color-coded bar)
- **Family details form** — child name, parent name, membership number
- **Coach notes** — free-text field ("Mom mentioned kid is shy — flag for coach")
- **"Add to Membership"** button to complete enrollment
- **Print Welcome Packet** — sport-specific checklist of what to bring on day one

## Key Features

- **Always-visible sidebar** — Real-time program availability, "Filling Up!" alerts, and upcoming season start dates
- **Dual-mode toggle** — Switch instantly between family-facing and staff-facing views
- **Printable welcome packet** — Auto-generated per sport (e.g., soccer: shin guards, cleats, water bottle)
- **Tablet-first layout** — Optimized for 1024x768 landscape, touch-friendly controls
- **Spot tracking** — Visual bars show remaining capacity, color-coded by urgency

## Why It Matters

Front-desk interactions are one of the highest-conversion moments for YMCA programs — a family is already there, already interested. But today, staff navigate binders or slow internal systems. This prototype turns a **5-minute lookup into a 30-second match**, keeps families engaged with a visual flow they can follow along with, and sends them home with a printed packet so they show up prepared on day one.
