# Find Your Team

**AI-Powered Activity Matcher for Parents & Kids**

---

## What It Is

A mobile-first tool that helps families discover the right YMCA sports program through two paths: a guided visual questionnaire or a free-form AI chat. Parents and kids choose how they want to explore — and both paths lead to personalized recommendations.

## Who It's For

- **Parents** looking for after-school or weekend activities for their child
- **Kids** (with a dedicated "kid mode" that adapts all language and tone)

## How It Works

### Path A — Guided Flow

1. **Pick your child's age** (5-6 through 13+)
2. **Choose interests** — Team Sports, Individual Sports, Creative Movement, Water Sports, Outdoor Adventure, or Multi-Sport
3. **Find your YMCA** — Interactive San Diego map narrows down to nearby branches
4. **Experience level** — First-timer, experienced, or "show me everything"
5. **See matches** — Up to 4 recommended programs with details and one-tap signup

### Path B — AI Chat

A conversational assistant (powered by Google Gemini) that talks families through finding the right fit. It asks natural follow-up questions, understands preferences described in plain language, and recommends specific programs inline with the conversation.

## Key Features

- **Parent / Kid toggle** — Kids see playful emoji-rich language ("Pick For Me!"), parents see clear informational copy
- **Interactive San Diego map** — Tap a region, then pick your closest branch
- **AI chat with inline program cards** — Recommendations appear directly in conversation, not on a separate page
- **Smart filtering** — Matches on age, interests, location, and beginner-friendliness
- **18 real programs** across 14 YMCA San Diego branches

## Why It Matters

Most families drop off during long registration forms. This prototype replaces the traditional catalog-and-form experience with something that feels like getting a recommendation from a friend — whether through quick visual steps or a real conversation. The goal: **reduce the gap between "I'm interested" and "I'm signed up."**
