# Every Kid Needs a Team

**QR-to-Signup Cinematic Landing Page**

---

## What It Is

A high-impact landing page designed to convert a single QR code scan into a program signup. Think of it as a digital poster — visually bold, emotionally compelling, and frictionless. One scan, two questions, three fields, done.

## Who It's For

- **First-time families** encountering YMCA programs through physical marketing (posters, flyers, event banners)
- **Marketing teams** looking for a measurable, scannable call-to-action

## How It Works

1. **Scan the QR code** — placed on posters, flyers, or event materials
2. **Cinematic hero** — Full-screen "Every Kid Needs a Team" with parallax scroll effect
3. **Why This Matters** — Animated data points build trust (72% lower depression risk, 3x social skills, 1 in 3 lack access)
4. **Quick match** — Just 2 questions: child's age + San Diego area
5. **See programs** — Matched results appear instantly
6. **Sign up** — 3-field form (your name, email, child's name)
7. **Confirmation** — Personalized welcome message ("We're excited to have Sofia on the team!")

## Key Features

- **Parallax hero** with staggered entrance animations — designed to feel premium, not institutional
- **Animated stat counters** — Numbers count up on scroll to create engagement
- **2-question matching** — Minimal friction; age + region is enough to surface relevant programs
- **3-field signup** — Name, email, child's name. Nothing more.
- **Built-in QR code** — The page generates its own QR code for printing and redistribution
- **Fully responsive** — Optimized for mobile (the most likely scan target)

## Why It Matters

YMCA programs are often discovered through word of mouth or physical presence at a branch. This prototype creates a **bridge between the physical and digital world** — a poster on a gym wall or a flyer at a school event becomes a direct signup funnel. Every scan is measurable, every signup is instant, and the experience feels nothing like filling out a form.
